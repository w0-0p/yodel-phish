Yodel Phish used a selected subset of 500 screenshots, added logo bounding-box
annotations, created a deterministic 400/100 training/validation split, and
exported a fine-tuned logo detector. The original dataset is not redistributed
with the Yodel Phish extension or source repository. No endorsement is
implied.
